Here is a list of terms that will prove useful while you navigate the repository.



Integrated Circuit (IC)
---
It is fabricated on top of silicon and has miniscule component (transistors, resistors, etc.) and is the foundation of modern electronic circuits.

Printed Circuit Board (PCB)
---
It is used as support and electrical connectivity between components in a device.
